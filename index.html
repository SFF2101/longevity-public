Longevity Drive

Longevity Drive is a private, non-commercial application for preserving an individual's personal health-data archives and derived datasets in Google Drive.

It is designed to copy local archival files to a dedicated application-managed Google Drive location, verify uploaded file integrity, and retain the local originals. It is not a public service, and no health records are published on this website.

Google Drive access

Longevity Drive requests only the Google Drive drive.file OAuth scope. This permits access to files created by the application or specifically authorised for it; it does not grant general access to unrelated Google Drive files.

Google Drive synchronisation is under development and has not yet been activated for personal health records.
